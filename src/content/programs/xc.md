## Ride all year with friends

Flyers XC keeps riders on their bikes between fall seasons. Winter rides build a base, spring practices get riders ready for races if they want them, and summer rides head up into the mountains. You don't need to be fast, and you don't need to race.

### How groups work

Riders are split into small groups by pace and skill, each with a volunteer ride leader and a parent sweep:

- **Trail confidence groups** work on smooth shifting, climbing traction, cornering position, and how to ride well in a group.
- **Race and endurance groups** add intervals, line choice through rocky sections, race starts, and pacing for Utah XC series races like the Intermountain Cup (ICUP).

### Taking care of our trails

Riders help maintain the trails they ride. Flyers XC riders and families join seasonal trail days on local Utah trails with trail groups and other families.
