## Built for Progression—Without the Private Club Price Tag

Many competitive youth development programs charge thousands of dollars per season. **Flyers XC** takes the opposite approach: our coaches and ride leaders are **100% volunteers**, and community sponsors cover club operations so riders only pay **$50** to cover their club t-shirt and rider insurance.

### How Groups Are Structured

Every practice splits into pace- and skill-matched pods led by trained volunteer coaches and parent sweeps:

- **Development & Trail Confidence Pods**: Focus on smooth shifting, climbing traction, cornering body position, and group-ride etiquette.
- **Race & Endurance Pods**: Structured intervals, technical rock-garden line selection, race starts, nutrition pacing, and support at regional Utah XC series events like the Intermountain Cup (ICUP).

### Community & Stewardship First

Riders learn that fast trails don't maintain themselves. Flyers XC athletes participate in seasonal trail stewardship days on local Utah trail networks alongside trail associations and families.
