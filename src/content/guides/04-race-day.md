Racing is optional at Flyers. If your rider wants to try it, here's how a race day usually goes.

## The night before

- Do the pre-ride bike check and fix anything now, not at the start line.
- Pack the bag: helmet, shoes, gloves, water, snacks, layers, sunscreen, and a few basic tools.
- Check the start time for your rider's category and plan to arrive early.

## At the venue

- Arrive at least an hour before your rider's start.
- Pick up the number plate and attach it to the bike.
- Find the Flyers team tent. It's where teammates and coaches meet.
- Pre-ride the course if the race allows it, or walk the hardest sections.

## Before the start

- Eat a small snack and drink water.
- Warm up with 15 to 20 minutes of easy riding.
- Get to the start area a few minutes early.

## During and after

Ride your own pace. Most riders start too fast. After the finish, drink, eat, and cheer for teammates who are still racing.

Every finish counts. We celebrate effort, not just the podium.
