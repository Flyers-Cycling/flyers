---
title: Food and water for rides
summary: What to eat and drink before, during, and after a ride, especially in Utah's dry air.
order: 3
---

Riders who run out of food or water have a hard day, even on easy trails. Utah's air is dry and many of our trails are high, so you lose water faster than you'd think.

## Before the ride

Eat a normal meal 2 to 3 hours before. If the ride is soon after school, a small snack an hour before helps.

Start drinking water early in the day, not just at the trailhead.

## During the ride

- Bring a full water bottle or hydration pack on every ride.
- Take a few sips every 10 to 15 minutes, before you feel thirsty.
- For rides longer than about an hour, bring a snack you like and can eat quickly, like a granola bar, fruit, or pretzels.
- On hot days, a sports drink helps replace what you sweat out.

## After the ride

Drink water and eat a snack or a meal soon after you finish. It helps your legs recover for the next ride.

## Sun

Wear sunscreen, even on cool days. The sun is strong at elevation.
