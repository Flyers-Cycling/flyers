---
title: Check your bike before every ride
summary: A two-minute check at the car catches most problems before they end a ride.
order: 1
---

Most flats, dropped chains, and brake problems show up in the parking lot if you look. Do this check before every ride. It takes two minutes.

## Air

Squeeze each tire. It should feel firm, not squishy. The range of pressures your tire can take is printed on its side. If you're not sure what pressure to run, ask a coach.

## Brakes

Squeeze each brake lever. It should stop well before it touches the handlebar. Push the bike forward with both brakes on. The wheels shouldn't roll.

## Chain and gears

Spin the pedals backward. The chain should run quietly and look clean, not rusty or caked in mud. Shift through a few gears while you pedal.

## Wheels and bolts

Check that both wheel axles or quick releases are tight. Lift the bike a few inches and drop it. Listen for rattles.

## Helmet

A helmet only works if it fits:

- It sits level, about two fingers above the eyebrows.
- The side straps make a V just below each ear.
- The chin strap is snug. One finger should fit under it.

Replace a helmet after any crash where it hit the ground, even if it looks fine.

If anything feels wrong, show a coach before the ride starts.
