Prep for the 2027 dirt season starts this winter! **Flyers Enduro** registration runs December through January (`$150` covering team t-shirt and gravity rider insurance), while **Flyers XC** opens for winter base miles and spring ICUP prep (`$50` covering club t-shirt and rider insurance). Both programs remain **100% volunteer-led** and built to complement fall NICA teams across Westlake, Lehi, Cedar Valley, Skyridge, and Lone Peak.
