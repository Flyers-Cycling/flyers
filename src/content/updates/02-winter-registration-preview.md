The 2027 season starts this winter. **Flyers Enduro** registration runs December through January, and **Flyers XC** sign-ups are open now for winter base rides and spring race prep. Both programs wrap up in August, before the fall school season starts.

Each program page lists the cost, what it covers, and how to ask for help if cost is a barrier.
